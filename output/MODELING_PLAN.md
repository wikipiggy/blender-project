# Contemporary Japanese courtyard villa

## Source and scale
The supplied `japanese villa.jpg` is the primary composition reference. No dimensioned drawing or separate space schedule is present. Dimensions below are design assumptions, not measured survey data.

- Single storey; enclosed footprint approximately 12 × 9 m.
- Floor raised 0.45 m; clear interior height approximately 2.8 m.
- Broad shallow hip roof: 15.2 × 11.6 m, eave at 3.55 m and ridge at 5.05 m.
- Front elevation: screened left wing approximately 5 m; recessed central passage/courtyard approximately 4 m; glazed right living bay approximately 3 m.
- A planted court behind the front engawa creates the nested frame perspective in the sketch.
- Only immediate garden and boundary elements; no mountains or distant landscape.

## Architectural context
- Kengo Kuma, Nezu Museum, Tokyo: low sweeping roof edges, deep shelter, bamboo and shaded transitions. https://kkaa.co.jp/en/project/nezu-museum/
- Kengo Kuma, Water/Cherry: thin roof expression and close relationship between dwelling, terraces and water. https://kkaa.co.jp/en/project/water-cherry/
- Tadao Ando, Chichu Art Museum: controlled courtyard light and restrained mineral surfaces. https://benesse-artsite.jp/en/art/chichu.html
These are conceptual precedents from established architectural knowledge. Attempts to access project websites during this task were blocked by the environment proxy (403); page contents and images were not retrieved. The supplied sketch takes priority over precedents.

## Modeling sequence
1. Establish a shallow hipped roof, raised plinth, asymmetric facade and courtyard opening.
2. Detail cedar screens, exposed posts and beams, soffit boards, roof seams, bronze window frames and translucent glazing.
3. Furnish the living and private rooms at human scale; add shoji and warm indirect lighting.
4. Build stone steps, irregular paving, gravel, moss, basin, sculptural rocks, bamboo and maple trees.
5. Create procedural roughness and bump for timber, plaster, stone, gravel and roof metal.
6. Render a sketch-comparison front view, inspect a progress image, refine geometry/materials, and produce final front, garden and courtyard images.
